# 3D model attribution

`minion-a01.glb`, `minion-b01.glb`, `minion-c01.glb` and `minion-d01.glb`
are CC0 1.0 models from Gobkit's free 3D asset library. These files were
provided by the project owner; the embedded GLB metadata identifies the
source and license. The A model is the player; B, C and D are reused for
the seven street visitors.
